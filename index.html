01 The idea

Magic with a
grammar.

Regnum is a programming-magic mod for Minecraft. Players author a spell by placing sigils on a circle, then the core runtime compiles that circle into a typed, bounded program.

Valid programs become server-authoritative effects. Invalid ones fail at the exact sigil that broke the grammar and can collapse into bounded Wild Magic. The surrounding repos build the identity, combat, progression, world, admin, and modpack layers around that shared language.

✦ Inspired by hard science and high fantasy.
