01 / WHAT IT IS

A mod, plus
the mods around it.

Vector-Regnum owns the spell language, the runtime, and the mana rules. The other six mods each handle one part of the SMP and call into it instead of copying it.

What that means playing it

Draw the circle wrong and the game points at the sigil that broke.

05 / WHERE IT STANDS

Playable,
not released.

The Fabric alpha runs: typed execution, the circle editor, mana and crystals, the field manual, multiplayer rules, automation, and the effects layer. That build is frozen now. Porting all of it to NeoForge is the current job.
